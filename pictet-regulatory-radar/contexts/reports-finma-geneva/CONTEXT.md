# FINMA Regulatory Reports — Geneva HQ

> Inventory of regulatory reports filed by Banque Pictet & Cie SA to FINMA. Update when filing obligations change.

## Reports

| Report | Frequency | Owner | Filing channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capital adequacy return (CAO) | Quarterly | Group Finance | FINMA EHP | Solo and consolidated |
| Liquidity return (LCR/NSFR) | Monthly | Group Finance | FINMA EHP | Consolidated |
| Large exposures return | Quarterly | Group Risk | FINMA EHP | Per counterparty |
| Interest rate risk in the banking book | Semi-annual | Group Risk | FINMA EHP | IRRBB per FINMA Circ. 2019/02 |
| MROS suspicious activity reports | Ad-hoc | AML Unit (Geneva) | goAML portal | Within 3 business days of suspicion |
| Annual regulatory audit report | Annual | Group Compliance | Direct to FINMA | Prepared by external auditors (art. 24 FINMASA) |
| Recovery plan | Annual (or on trigger) | Group Finance + Group Risk | Direct to FINMA | Updated annually; ad-hoc on trigger events |
