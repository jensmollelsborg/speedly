## §1 — Cross-Border Activities Policy (v4.2, effective 2024-03-15)

### 3.1 Client Identification

For clients domiciled in jurisdictions designated "higher risk" for cross-border solicitation (including Hong Kong SAR, Singapore, mainland China, UAE, and the list maintained at Annex A), the relationship manager must conduct in-person identification at a Pictet office or via a video-verified procedure pre-approved by Compliance.

### 3.2 Solicitation Restrictions

Active solicitation of prospects in a foreign jurisdiction is permitted only where Pictet holds the requisite licence or recognised exemption in that jurisdiction. The Cross-Border Country Manual (Annex B) sets out per-jurisdiction rules. Where the Country Manual is silent, the activity is **prohibited** pending review by Cross-Border Compliance.

### 3.3 Travel & Booking

RMs travelling to a client jurisdiction must register the trip in the Travel Pre-Approval System at least 5 business days before departure, attaching the planned client agenda. Unannounced visits are prohibited.

## §2 — AML / Customer Due Diligence Policy (v6.1, effective 2025-01-01)

### 4.1 Standard CDD

Standard CDD is required for every new client relationship and consists of: (a) identification of the client, beneficial owners and controlling persons; (b) verification using independent documentation; (c) understanding of the purpose and intended nature of the relationship; (d) source-of-funds and source-of-wealth documentation.

### 4.2 Enhanced CDD

Enhanced CDD applies where any of the following is true: PEP status, high-risk jurisdiction nexus, complex ownership structure, cash-intensive business as source of wealth, or aggregate AUM exceeding CHF 25m. Enhanced CDD requires senior management approval at onboarding and annual review.

### 8.0 Third-Party Payments

Inbound and outbound payments involving third parties (i.e. parties other than the client or a documented beneficiary) require pre-approval. Payments routed through unregulated payment service providers are prohibited regardless of amount.

## §3 — Suitability & Appropriateness Policy (v3.0, effective 2024-09-01)

### 2.4 Suitability for Discretionary Mandates

A discretionary mandate must be aligned with the client's risk profile, investment objectives, financial situation, knowledge, and experience as documented in the Client Investment Profile. Profile reviews are required (a) every 24 months, (b) on material life-event notification, and (c) on RM change.

### 2.7 Complex Products

Products classified "Complex" under the FinSA/MiFID II taxonomy (structured products, leveraged instruments, certain alternative funds) require an appropriateness test before execution-only orders are accepted from advisory or execution clients.

## §4 — Data Protection & Confidentiality (v2.3, effective 2024-06-01)

### 5.0 Client Data Outside Switzerland

Client data may be transferred outside Switzerland only to a Pictet group entity or to a contractually bound third party, and only where the receiving jurisdiction is recognised by the Swiss FDPIC as offering adequate protection, or where appropriate safeguards (SCCs) are in place. Transfers to the United States require a documented impact assessment.

### 7.0 Generative AI and Confidential Data

Confidential client data, including names, account numbers, and identifiable transaction details, must **not** be entered into public generative AI tools (including public versions of ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude). Pictet-approved internal AI environments are listed at Annex D and must be used for any AI-assisted work involving client data.

## §5 — Code of Conduct (v5.0, effective 2025-02-01)

### 6.0 Personal Account Dealing

Employees must execute personal securities trades through approved Pictet accounts or pre-cleared external accounts. Holding period: minimum 30 days for listed equities; advance pre-clearance required for any trade above CHF 10,000.

### 9.0 Gifts and Entertainment

Gifts received from clients or counterparties must be reported in the G&E Register if value exceeds CHF 100. Acceptance of gifts above CHF 300 requires line manager and Compliance approval.

## §6 — ESG Investment Policy (v1.4, effective 2025-04-01)

### 4.0 Exclusions

Pictet discretionary mandates exclude direct investment in: (a) companies deriving more than 25% of revenue from thermal coal mining; (b) controversial-weapons manufacturers (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, chemical/biological weapons); (c) tobacco producers above 5% revenue threshold.

### 6.0 Climate Disclosures

Discretionary portfolios above CHF 100m AUM produce an annual climate report covering portfolio carbon intensity (Scope 1+2, where available Scope 3), temperature alignment, and exposure to physical/transition risk above defined thresholds.
