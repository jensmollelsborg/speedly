# Pictet Asia Limited — Singapore Branch

> Reference context for skills that need to reason about the Singapore entity, its supervisor, or the regulatory returns it files. Update quarterly.

## Business activities

- Discretionary portfolio management for HNW and UHNW clients domiciled in SE Asia.
- Advisory portfolio management under MAS RA(2) regime.
- Wealth-planning support (booked through Geneva or Singapore at the client's election).
- Trust and fiduciary services delivered jointly with Pictet trust companies.
- *Out of scope:* retail banking, payment services, custody for unrelated counterparties.

## Regulator and licence

| | |
|---|---|
| **Supervisor** | Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) |
| **Licence** | Capital Markets Services (CMS) under the Securities and Futures Act (SFA) |
| **Regulated activities** | RA(2) Advising on Investment Products; RA(4) Dealing in Capital Markets Products; Custodial Services |
| **Last on-site review** | 2024-09 |

## Applicable regulatory reports

| Report | Frequency | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAS Form 1 — Capital Adequacy | Monthly | Finance Singapore | Filed via MASNET; CET1 + LCR |
| MAS Notice SFA 04-N02 returns | Quarterly | Risk Singapore | Market risk + large exposures |
| MAS Notice 626 — AML/CFT report | Annual | Compliance Singapore | Includes self-assessment against the FATF Recommendations |
| Suspicious Transaction Reports | Ad-hoc | Compliance Singapore | Filed within 5 business days of suspicion |
| MAS Notice SFA 04-N09 — Outsourcing register | On change | Operations Singapore | Maintained per Outsourcing Guidelines 2018 |

## Internal owners (single source of truth)

- **Branch Head:** Compliance Singapore is the relationship owner for MAS dialogue.
- **Finance returns:** Finance Singapore, escalating to Group Finance.
- **Risk returns:** Risk Singapore, escalating to Group Risk.
- **AML / STR:** Compliance Singapore, escalating to the AML Unit (Geneva).
- **Outsourcing register:** Operations Singapore, with IT Risk for tech vendors.
